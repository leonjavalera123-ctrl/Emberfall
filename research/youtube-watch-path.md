# Leon's Watch Path — 12 verified videos

Every URL was verified live (title + channel confirmed) on 2026-08-17. Lengths marked "~" are approximate; items flagged "length unverified" exist but runtime wasn't confirmed.

## Phase 1 — Game feel: why polish makes cheap games feel expensive
1. **Secrets of Game Feel and Juice** — Game Maker's Toolkit, ~6 min — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=216_5nu4aVQ — the fastest primer on why identical mechanics can feel flat or fantastic; vocabulary for everything that follows.
2. **Juice it or lose it — Martin Jonasson & Petri Purho** — grapefrukt, ~15 min — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fy0aCDmgnxg — THE classic: a boring Breakout clone transformed live, one tween/particle/shake at a time. Directly transferable to unit deaths and building explosions in Emberfall.
3. **The Art of Screenshake — Jan Willem Nijman (Vlambeer)** — Dutch Game Garden, ~16 min — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AJdEqssNZ-U — 30 concrete tweaks (muzzle flash, hit-stop, camera kick) applied live to a dull shooter; a literal checklist to hand to Claude as polish tickets.

## Phase 2 — Making Godot 3D actually look good
4. **Lighting in Godot for Beginners** — Brackeys, ~15 min — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aRdiiWpA0AA — light nodes, shadows, environment; the single highest-leverage visual upgrade for an RTS map.
5. **Learning Godot 4.x — 3D Lighting and Shadows, Global Illumination, World Environments** — GameFromScratch, ~25 min — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xmykSGbq7AE — the deeper pass: SDFGI/VoxelGI/lightmaps, WorldEnvironment, tonemapping, glow, SSAO.

## Phase 3 — Animation fundamentals for units
6. **12 Principles of Animation (Official Full Series)** — AlanBeckerTutorials, ~24 min — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uDqjIdI4bF4 — squash/stretch, anticipation, timing; the vocabulary to judge (and prompt-fix) generated unit animations.
7. **Animation Bootcamp: An Indie Approach to Procedural Animation** — GDC, ~30 min — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LNidsMesxSE — David Rosen (Overgrowth) gets fluid characters from ~13 keyframes; the ideal cheap-animation philosophy for a solo dev.

## Phase 4 — AI-assisted asset pipelines (recent; smaller channels, verified live)
8. **How to Create Game Assets With AI in 2026 — Full Guide** — Roboverse, length unverified — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AQyYv5gknWs — image → 3D → engine flow, close to the existing Higgsfield/Hunyuan pipeline; good for stealing refinements.
9. **How To Make AI Games Using Claude + Higgsfield (Full Tutorial)** — Skai Generated, length unverified — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8Neoro2wyZU — a Claude + Higgsfield workflow end to end; a process comparison against the current Claude Code loop.

## Phase 5 — RTS wisdom from the source
10. **Classic Game Postmortem: Command & Conquer** — GDC, ~60 min — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rIXK3fRx4Zo — Westwood's Louis Castle and the original C&C team on how Red Alert's direct ancestor was designed, scoped, and shipped.
- *Bonus, no confirmed YouTube URL:* Dustin Browder, **"The Game Design of StarCraft II: Designing an E-Sport"** (GDC 2011) — free on GDC Vault (gdcvault.com/play/1014488); search YouTube for `GDC Game Design of StarCraft II e-sport`. The best talk ever given on RTS unit readability and faction clarity.

## Phase 6 — Starting Unreal Engine 5
11. **Godot 4 Vs Unreal Engine 5: Which Game Engine Should You Use?** — Matt Aspland, short (length unverified) — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wovBxJwl2BQ — watch before investing hours.
12. **Unreal Engine 5 Beginner Tutorial — UE5 Starter Course** — Unreal Sensei, multi-hour — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-zMkzmduqI — the de-facto standard UE5 on-ramp; do it in 30-minute chunks.

## Suggested order
1 → 2 → 3 in one evening (under 40 min total, immediately actionable on Emberfall) · 4 → 5 next session · 6 → 7 before the next unit-animation pass · 8 → 9 casually · 10 as a weekend documentary · 11 → 12 only when genuinely ready to open UE5.
